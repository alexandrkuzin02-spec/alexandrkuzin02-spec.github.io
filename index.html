Claude Helper - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Claude Helper is a personal tool used only by its owner, Alexandr Kuzin, to manage his own Pinterest account (boards and pins).

1. What data we access

Only the owner's own Pinterest data through the official Pinterest API: profile information, boards and pins.

2. How we use data

Only to organize boards, find ideas and create pins for the owner's account.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

4. Storage

We do not store Pinterest data permanently. The access token is kept in secure storage and can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings.

5. Other users

The app does not collect data from any other Pinterest users.
